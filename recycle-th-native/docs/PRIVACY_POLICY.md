# Privacy Policy for Recycle Terre Haute

**Last Updated: September 2025**

## Introduction

Recycle Terre Haute ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Images**: Photos you take or upload for recycling analysis
- **Location Data**: Your location when you enable location services
- **Account Information**: Email address if you create an account
- **Usage Data**: Your scanning history and app interactions

### Automatically Collected Information
- Device type and operating system
- App usage statistics
- Crash reports and performance data

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Identify recyclable items through image analysis
- Find nearby recycling centers based on your location
- Send collection day reminders
- Improve our recycling identification accuracy
- Provide customer support
- Analyze app usage to improve features

## Data Sharing

We share your information only:
- With OpenAI for image analysis (images are processed and not stored)
- With emergency services if required by law
- With your explicit consent

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with advertisers
- Store images after analysis (unless you save them to history)

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Secure storage using industry standards
- Regular security audits
- Limited access to personal data

## Data Retention

- **Images**: Deleted immediately after analysis unless saved to history
- **History**: Stored locally on your device for 30 days
- **Location**: Used in real-time, not stored
- **Account Data**: Retained until account deletion

## Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's information.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Opt-out of data collection
- Export your data

## Location Services

Location data is used to:
- Find nearest recycling centers
- Provide directions to facilities
- Send geofence reminders (optional)

You can disable location services at any time in your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

We use:
- **OpenAI API**: For image recognition (see OpenAI's privacy policy)
- **Apple Maps**: For directions (see Apple's privacy policy)
- **Expo**: For app infrastructure (see Expo's privacy policy)

## California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under CCPA including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt-out.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and through in-app notifications for material changes.
