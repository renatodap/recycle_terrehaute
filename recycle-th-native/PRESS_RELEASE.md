## New Mobile App Makes Recycling Simple for Terre Haute Residents

### AI-Powered "SnapCycle" App Launches to Support City's Sustainability Goals

**TERRE HAUTE, IN – [DATE]** – A new mobile application designed specifically for Terre Haute and Vigo County residents promises to take the guesswork out of recycling. "SnapCycle," available free on iOS and Android devices, uses artificial intelligence to instantly identify items and provide local recycling guidance.

The app addresses a common frustration: uncertainty about what can be recycled and where. Users simply point their phone camera at any item to receive immediate, accurate recycling instructions based on Vigo County's specific regulations.

"Every community has different recycling rules," said [YOUR NAME], creator of SnapCycle. "What's recyclable in Indianapolis might not be accepted here. This app gives residents confidence they're recycling correctly according to our local guidelines."

### Key Features Include:

- **Smart Scanner Technology**: AI-powered camera instantly identifies items and materials
- **Local Facility Finder**: Directions to Vigo County Solid Waste Management, ISU recycling points, and specialized drop-off locations
- **24/7 Recycling Assistant**: Answers questions about any recycling topic
- **Collection Reminders**: Customizable notifications ensure users never miss recycling day
- **Environmental Impact Tracking**: Shows users their contribution to sustainability

The app covers all Vigo County areas including Indiana State University campus, Rose-Hulman vicinity, and West Terre Haute. It handles guidance for all material types from common plastics to specialized disposal for electronics and hazardous materials.

### Supporting Local Sustainability

The launch aligns with Terre Haute's ongoing environmental initiatives and supports the city's waste reduction goals. The app specifically incorporates information from Vigo County Solid Waste Management District and local recycling facilities.

"Making recycling easier and more accessible is crucial for our community's environmental future," said [LOCAL OFFICIAL NAME/TITLE - to be added]. "This tool helps residents make informed decisions about waste disposal."

### Privacy-First Approach

The app can be used without creating an account, addressing privacy concerns while still providing full functionality. Users who choose to create accounts can sync their data across devices and access additional features.

### Availability

SnapCycle is available now as a free download on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Premium features, including unlimited scans and advanced analytics, are available through optional in-app purchases.

The app was developed using React Native technology and integrates with OpenAI for image recognition, ensuring accurate and continually improving recycling guidance.

### About

SnapCycle is dedicated to making recycling simple and effective for all Vigo County residents. The app represents months of research into local recycling regulations and collaboration with environmental experts.
